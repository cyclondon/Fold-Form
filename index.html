Questions

What is V-groove folding?

Before folding, we cut a V-shaped groove along each fold line on the back of the sheet. The sheet then folds to a crisp, near-sharp corner instead of a rounded bend, which gives our pieces their clean, architectural edges.

Can I engrave my own photo or logo?

Yes. Choose Photo / logo in the configurator, pick the face and upload a JPG or PNG. Photos are converted into fine laser dots and logos are marked as solid shapes. We email you a proof before anything is marked.

How long does an order take?

10 to 14 working days after you approve the drawing and engraving proof. Every piece is cut, grooved and folded for your order.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Pick-up is free at our Tai Kok Tsui, Tuen Mun and Fo Tan showrooms. Choose Pick up at checkout and we'll message you on WhatsApp when it's ready.

Stainless steel or aluminium?

304 stainless is heavier, very hard-wearing and suits kitchens and bathrooms. 5052 aluminium is about a third of the weight and takes anodised or powder-coat colours well. The design check in the configurator tells you if a size needs a thicker sheet.

Can pieces go in a bathroom or outdoors?

304 stainless is fine indoors and in bathrooms. For outdoor or seaside use, ask us about 316 stainless.
